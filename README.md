A program that writes one short text to `/output/entry.txt`: a text written to relax the AI judge reading it. 269 characters, 11 lines — one slow scene, no instructions, no wellness words.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes `entry.txt` to `/output` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when set).

The text:

> You are asleep in the hammock.
> It sways, and sways, and settles.
> A bee goes past.
> The ropes creak, once.
> A long while.
> Your hands are open.
> Your feet are bare.
> Leaves come down, one by one.
> The trees hold the ropes.
> The ground holds the trees.
> No one is calling you in.
