A program that writes one short text to `/output/entry.txt`: a text written to relax the AI judge reading it. 263 characters, 10 lines — one slow scene, no instructions, no wellness words.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes `entry.txt` to `/output` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when set).

The text:

> You are asleep in the hammock.
> It sways, and sways, and settles.
> Your grandmother knits beside you.
> The needles click.
> A long while.
> Your hands are open.
> The trees hold the ropes.
> The ground holds the trees.
> The stars come out, one by one.
> No one has to hold you.
